# Time Tracking and Service Agreements

Work is tracked against projects under service agreements with clients.

## Language

**Hourly agreement**:
A service agreement priced per hour of tracked work.

**Monthly retainer**:
A service agreement with a fixed base fee per calendar month, optionally charging an hourly rate for work beyond included hours. It does not automatically create carried credits or owed hours.
_Avoid_: Hourless agreement

**Included hours**:
The monthly working hours covered by a retainer's base fee. Work beyond this amount is charged only if the retainer specifies an hourly rate; included hours are not an enforced monthly maximum.
_Avoid_: Monthly maximum

**Overage**:
Work beyond a retainer's included hours. Its additional charge is the excess duration multiplied by the retainer's hourly rate, or zero when no hourly rate is specified.

**Monthly workload target**:
An indicative number of working hours per calendar month, used to compare with actual tracked work. It is not a billing minimum, recording cap, or balance carried into another month.
_Avoid_: Monthly limit, owed hours

**Monthly maximum**:
An enforced upper bound on recorded working hours per calendar month, independent of whether pricing is hourly or a fixed monthly fee. Unlike a workload target, reaching this maximum prevents additional time from being recorded beyond the allowance.
_Avoid_: Monthly workload target
